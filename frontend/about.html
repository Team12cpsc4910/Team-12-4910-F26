How it works

  1. 1 Drivers join a sponsor

    Drivers apply to a sponsor company's incentive program.

  2. 2 Sponsors award points

    Sponsors add points for good driving and record the reason.

  3. 3 Drivers redeem rewards

    Drivers spend points on rewards from their sponsor's catalog.
